Prefix-cache correction
- The first full matrix exposed that this vLLM dev image defaults
  enable_prefix_caching=True even when the CLI flag is omitted
- The original long-context files and a unique-seed diagnostic rerun observed cache
  hits and are excluded from the formal aggregate
- The service was restarted with --no-enable-prefix-caching; runtime metrics then
  reported enable_prefix_caching=False
- Formal 32K/64K/130K results use new seeds and num_warmups=0; all first-shape
  effects are retained
